B) Headless: UNITY=/path/to/Unity ./generate_assets.sh   (Windows: generate_assets.bat)
Then: CyberDefense > Validate Project; open Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity; Play.
Problems: CyberDefense > Diagnose Project; send me any red Console errors or generate_*.log.
